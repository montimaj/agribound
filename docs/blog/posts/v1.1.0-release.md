# Agribound v1.1.0: Revised Stitching and Fine-Tuning, New Sources, a SAM 2.1 Engine, and the Examples Run Again

<!-- Draft until the release: set the date and remove `draft: true` then. -->

agribound 1.1.0 changes what several components produce for the same
configuration, adds sources, two engines (crop-history zones and SAM 2.1
automatic masks), opt-in options and evaluation options, and reports what the
examples give when they are run again, with three evaluations whose rules
were fixed before they ran. Outputs of agribound 1.0.1
made by the changed components are no longer reused; please read the
[list of affected results](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md#results-produced-with-agribound-101-that-are-affected)
before reusing them.

<!-- more -->

## What changes results

The first four raise a results version: an output that an earlier release
made with one of them is refused (`FileExistsError`) until it is made again
with `overwrite=True` (CLI `--overwrite`). The others say below when an
output is reused. The new engine `sam-auto`, the centre-pivot snapping and
DINOv3's half-band growth (below) are opt-in: without them, outputs,
configuration hashes and results versions are unchanged.

- **Delineate-Anything tile stitching** (the native backend). A detection
  counts as cut by its tile when it reaches within 4 px of an interior tile
  edge, contested area goes to complete detections first, and pieces that
  meet along a tile edge are joined when they pass upstream DelAnyFlow's seam
  test. At Twifo Praso (example 23, SPOT 6/7 panchromatic), the share of the
  boundary within 2 m of a tile edge fell from 14.0 % to 12.7 % (2.1 % by
  chance) and the split rate of the estate blocks from 0.49 to 0.44. The
  seam test also joins some neighbouring fields, and other artefacts remain
  (below).
- **Delineate-Anything fine-tuning** (recipe 3): no warmup learning rate for
  the biases, chips half a chip apart split by chip-size cells, an optimizer
  step after every batch, and no labels for slivers cut by the chip frame. At
  Oro (example 23), the share of the output boundary on the tile grid fell
  from 13.7 % to 3.2 % (1.9 % by chance), recall rose from 0.27 to 0.42, and
  the merge rate from 0.20 to 0.49.
- **DINOv3 fine-tuning and inference**: recipe 2 (class weights, the
  checkpoint with the best validation mean IoU, the backbone's input
  normalisation, a 4 px boundary band) and blended inference windows. On the
  Twifo Praso square, the model fine-tuned on the NORPALM estate draws 102
  polygons and matches 36 of the 55 blocks; with agribound 1.0.1 it drew one
  polygon over 94 % of the square.
- **DINOv3 on rasters coarser than 10 m** reads a 10 m bilinear copy. On
  example 14's square the Landsat composite then gives 52 training chips
  instead of 4, and the in-sample F1 rose from 0.014 to 0.534 (HLS: 0.547).
- **TESSERA**: the v1 store was converted again upstream on 2026-10-06/08,
  which moved most tiles by one 10 m pixel; in examples 02 and 15 the
  rasters now lie within about 1 m of the Sentinel-2 composites, where they
  lay 9-10 m east or south before. agribound now records the state of the
  store objects it reads and reads a cached raster again when they change.
  An output is refused when the objects it was read from have changed since
  or their state was not recorded; without network access, only when its
  cached raster has been read again since.
- **HLS**: the HLSS30 images of the MGRS tiles at 180° were counted
  everywhere (example 03 recorded 298 images; 77 are real) and were missing
  from composites inside those tiles. Away from 180° the pixels do not
  change and outputs are reused; outputs whose study area lies at those
  tiles are refused.
- **Export grids that reach across 180°** (a study area that ends at 180° or
  up to about 10 km short of it, every Earth Engine source) selected images
  all around the globe at their latitude: for a 5 km square in Fiji, 20,806
  HLS images instead of the 76 of its tile. The image counts and years of
  such composites change, and for HLS their pixels can too; composites of
  the other sources cached before are reused until you delete them.

## What 1.1.0 adds

- **Sources:** `landsat-pan` (Landsat panchromatic, 15 m); `sentinel1`
  (Sentinel-1 GRD composites); `umbra` and `capella` (open X-band SAR from
  the AWS Registry of Open Data, composited locally without Earth Engine);
  `cdl-sequence` and `aci-sequence` (stacks of annual crop-type maps).
- **Crop-history zones:** the `crop-sequence` engine makes zones of uniform
  multi-year crop history, the way USDA NASS makes its Crop Sequence
  Boundaries; `attach_crop_history` adds the crop history to any outlines,
  and `query_csb` reads the published Crop Sequence Boundaries. The zones
  are not field outlines.
- **SAM 2.1 automatic masks:** the `sam-auto` engine runs Meta's Segment
  Anything 2.1 (large) automatic mask generation, a grid of point prompts,
  on large overlapping tiles, and turns the masks into one set of
  non-overlapping polygons, with no field labels and no fine-tuning. It is
  opt-in: never an engine default, and the agent proposes it only when it is
  named. Its settings per source were chosen on two Nevada squares: defaults
  for NAIP and SPOT 6/7; Sentinel-2 and Landsat run with a WARNING about
  their GPU cost; Sentinel-1 has no default. The new `sam-auto` extra
  installs `sam2` 1.1.0, a third-party PyPI build of Meta's code whose 62
  code, configuration and licence files equal Meta's commit 2b90b9f (SAM 2.1
  code and weights: Meta, Apache-2.0), without segment-geospatial. HPC
  tiling refuses the engine.
- **Evaluation:** chance baselines (`chance_baselines`,
  `evaluate(..., baselines=...)`: a blind grid and a shifted reference),
  `evaluate(..., ignore=...)`, which leaves out of the score the predictions
  on excluded ground, and `paired_bootstrap`, paired intervals of the
  difference between two layers over reference fields or spatial blocks.
- **Dates in a composite:** a scene survey (`agribound scenes`), single-date
  composites, image-date tags and per-pixel date layers.
- **Options:** `lulc_tree_crops` for orchards and plantations;
  `processing_gsd_m` and `stretch_percentiles` for Delineate-Anything;
  centre-pivot snapping after any engine (`engine_params["pivots"]`), which
  rejoins pivots cut into pieces and snaps round polygons to circles; and,
  for DINOv3, `dilate_interior_px="half_band"`, which grows each field
  interior by half the boundary band measured on the prediction.
- **Examples 23 to 28:** tree crops in four countries, two Nevada basins,
  one square in each of 47 US states, radar at seven sites, crop-history
  zones in Quebec, and three New York landscapes against the Common Land
  Units of the OpenET field boundaries. Example 24 adds `--sam-auto` and
  `--options` (its runs with the opt-in options), example 25 `--sam-auto`.

## What was tested and not adopted

- **Fixes of the tile stitching.** The runs show where Delineate-Anything's
  stitching still goes wrong: seam joins of two orchard blocks whose road
  gap runs up to 21 m off the tile edge (the Madera F1 is 0.83, against
  0.86 with agribound 1.0.1's stitching), tile-cut detections chained into
  blocks across fields (up to 359.6 ha in the Montana square), a block of
  strip plots in the Nile Delta drawn as one polygon, and outlines that end
  on the edge of the last tile that detected a field. Candidate fixes were
  evaluated against rules set before the evaluation; each lowered F1 by more
  than 0.005 somewhere or put more boundary on tile edges, so none was
  adopted, and the [engines guide](../../user-guide/engines.md#delineate-anything-delineate-anything)
  describes these cases.
- **Superpixel segmentation (SNIC, SLIC)** as an engine for oil palm estate
  blocks: tested at four estates with a rule fixed in advance; no setting
  passed, so agribound has no superpixel engine (see
  [Superpixel segmentation](../../user-guide/satellite-sources.md#superpixel-segmentation-snic-slic)).
- **Growing DINOv3's polygons on 6 m SPOT, as a default.** In example 24 the
  model fine-tuned on SPOT draws polygons inside the field edges (Diamond
  Valley: boundary F1 0.07 with F1 0.50). Three growth rules set before they
  were run were tested; none met its bar, and the default growth is
  unchanged. A later rule, `dilate_interior_px="half_band"`, passed a
  pre-registered pilot and a test on three other Nevada squares, raising
  boundary F1 on 5 of 6 runs, the fewest its rule allowed (on SPOT 6/7 from
  0.07-0.09 to 0.28-0.33), and is an opt-in option.
- **`sam-auto` on Sentinel-2.** In the pre-registered evaluation it did not
  transfer (F1 0.152 below Delineate Anything v2 over 46 of example 25's
  squares). Its setting is unchanged and was not tried again; it stays
  available, never a default, with a WARNING that states its cost and this
  result.
- **SPOT 6/7 checkpoints fine-tuned on two squares instead of one** (Diamond
  Valley): reported only, from separate training runs with one seed each, and
  not part of 1.1.0.

## Upgrading

- Python >= 3.12 and the two environments of 1.0 (`environment.yml` with
  FTW, `environment-gfm.yml` with Prithvi).
- Outputs of the changed components raise `FileExistsError` until they are
  made again (`overwrite=True`, CLI `--overwrite`); cached fine-tuning runs
  of Delineate-Anything and DINOv3 are trained again.
- New extras `crop-sequence` and `sam-auto` (both in `all` and `all-gfm`).
- `composite_method="greenest"` or `"max_ndvi"` with `spot-pan` (or the new
  `landsat-pan` and `sentinel1`) raises `ValueError` when the configuration
  is created, not when the composite is built.
- The DINOv3 `trainer_kwargs` keys `class_weights`, `loss_fn`,
  `monitor_metric`, `mode` and `patience`, which are not Lightning `Trainer`
  arguments, raise `ValueError` and name the engine parameter to use instead
  (with agribound 1.0.1, `class_weights` and `loss_fn` reached geoai's
  training function and the other three raised `TypeError`).
- `usgs_retries` defaults to 5.

The CHANGELOG lists every change.

## What has not been verified

- The SAM 3 backends (`sam3`, `sam3-hf`) have not been run end to end (the
  weights are gated); agribound logs a WARNING when one is loaded.
- Example 01 was not run end to end, and of example 12 only the NAIP runs
  were. In those NAIP runs GeoAI scored lower than the 1.0 model at both
  references (F1 0.29 against 0.44 on V18); the labels, 16 more chips and
  the operating system differed, which of these lowered the score was not
  established, and the spread between trainings with other seeds was not
  measured.
- DINOv3's 10 m grid was not tested on Landsat PAN.
- `sam-auto` was evaluated on Apple MPS only. A run gives the same polygons on
  the same device with the same versions, and masks differ between devices
  (MPS against CPU: mask IoU 0.59-0.97 for the same fields, measured in the
  SAM 2 refinement stage); no score on CUDA or CPU is reported. The sources
  without a piloted setting
  (for example HLS, Landsat PAN, SPOT-Pan, USGS NAIP Plus, Umbra, Capella and
  local rasters) run at native scale with a WARNING and were not evaluated.
- In example 28's Black Dirt square the pre-registered chance baseline (the
  reference moved by half its median width) matches 470 of the 668 fields,
  so no run there can be shown above chance; a different baseline would need
  a new pre-registration.
- The agent with a language model (part B of example 18) was not run. Its
  first-party API path was tested with a mock transport, not against the
  live API, and Ollama and vLLM endpoints have not been tested.
- The Slurm scripts in `examples/hpc/` have been exercised with fake
  `sbatch`, `squeue` and `scancel` commands; a submission on a real
  scheduler was not part of the 1.0.0 checks, and the 1.1 changes record
  none.
